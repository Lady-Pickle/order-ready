When food is ready, staff tap Ready ✓ on their phone and the number appears on the TV by the counter, with a chime. No more calling out numbers over the noise. Try it: add a number on the phone, tap Ready ✓, and watch the TV.

This demo is shared, so other visitors may be trying it too, and numbers clear after 30 minutes. A real café gets its own copy, with a staff PIN so customers can't change it. It's on free hosting: if nobody has visited for a while, the first load takes a little longer.
